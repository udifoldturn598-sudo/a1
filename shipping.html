Shipping Policy

This page provides general shipping guidance for orders or partner fulfilment referenced through Wardrobe Alpha Edge.

Processing

Where an order or merchant checkout is available, processing times depend on the seller and the items selected. Orders may require additional time during launches, promotions, weekends or public holidays. A confirmation or tracking reference should be kept for your records.

Delivery

Delivery times vary by destination, carrier and service level. Address accuracy is the customer's responsibility. Carrier delays, weather, customs procedures and other events outside reasonable control can affect estimated delivery dates.

If a parcel appears delayed or the tracking information has not updated for an extended period, contact the relevant seller or carrier using the order reference so the shipment can be investigated.

International orders

International deliveries may be subject to customs clearance, import taxes, duties or local handling fees. These charges, where applicable, may be payable by the recipient unless the checkout explicitly states otherwise. Customs requirements vary by destination.

Damaged or incorrect deliveries

If an item arrives damaged, incorrect or materially different from the order, keep the packaging and contact the seller promptly with photographs and the order information. Do not discard the packaging until the carrier or merchant confirms the next step.
