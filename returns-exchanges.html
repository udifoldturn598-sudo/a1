Customer Care / Returns

Returns & Exchanges

Returns and exchanges should be requested promptly and according to the terms provided with the specific order.

Before returning

Check the order confirmation and seller instructions first. Confirm the return window, condition requirements and whether an exchange is available for the item, size or color you want.

Condition of items

Unless a product is faulty or local law provides otherwise, returned fashion items should generally be unworn, unwashed and free from avoidable damage, with tags and packaging retained where required. Try garments on carefully to avoid marks, odors or damage.

Exchanges

Where exchanges are offered, availability can change while a return is being processed. A merchant may recommend returning the original item and placing a new order instead. Follow the specific instructions provided with your purchase.

Faulty or incorrect items

If you receive a faulty or incorrect item, contact the seller as soon as reasonably possible and provide photographs, the order number and a description of the issue. Do not alter or repair the item before the merchant has reviewed the claim unless instructed to do so.
