Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how cookies and similar technologies may be used to keep the website functional, secure and useful.

Essential technologies

Some cookies or browser storage may be necessary for basic site functions, preferences, security and reliable navigation. These technologies are generally required for the website to operate as intended.

Analytics and optional technologies

Where analytics are enabled, optional technologies may collect information such as page views, approximate device characteristics, referral information and interactions. Optional analytics should be activated only where the applicable consent mechanism permits it.

We aim to keep analytics focused on improving the website rather than building detailed personal profiles. You can also use browser controls to restrict or remove cookies, although some preferences may then need to be selected again.

Managing cookies

Most modern browsers allow you to block, delete or limit cookies from their privacy settings. Blocking essential technologies can affect navigation or form functionality. If a consent banner is provided, its settings can also be used to manage optional technologies.
