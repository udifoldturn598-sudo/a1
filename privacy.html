Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Wardrobe Alpha Edge handles information when you browse the website, contact the editorial team, or use site features.

Information we may receive

We may receive information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address and message when you contact us. If a newsletter or preference form is enabled, we may also receive the information submitted through that form.

Standard technical information may be processed automatically, including browser type, device information, approximate location derived from network data, referring pages, pages viewed and basic interaction events. This information helps us understand site performance and improve the editorial experience.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, maintain website functionality, understand which editorial areas are useful, protect the website against abuse, and improve content and navigation. We do not use enquiry information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate reason or permission.

Where analytics are enabled, aggregated usage information may be used to understand traffic patterns and content performance. You can control optional analytics through the site's consent controls where available.

Sharing and service providers

We may use trusted hosting, analytics, email, security and technical service providers to operate the website. These providers should receive only the information necessary to perform their services and are expected to handle information appropriately.

We do not sell personal information as a business model. Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with a legal obligation, enforce site terms, protect users or protect the security and integrity of the website.

Retention and your choices

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for legitimate operational needs, or where a longer period is required by law. You may contact us to ask what personal information we hold about you or to request correction or deletion where applicable.
