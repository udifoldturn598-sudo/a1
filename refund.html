Refund Policy

This Refund Policy outlines the general approach to refunds for purchases connected with the website.

Eligibility

Refund eligibility depends on the applicable seller, product condition and any statutory consumer rights in the destination country. Items should normally be returned in the condition required by the seller, with original packaging or tags where applicable.

Refund process

Once a return is received and assessed, the merchant may approve, partially approve or decline a refund according to its published terms and applicable law. Approved refunds are normally sent back to the original payment method. Processing times can vary by payment provider and bank.

Non-returnable or restricted items

Some goods may be excluded from ordinary returns because of hygiene, personalization, damage caused after delivery, or other lawful restrictions. The specific merchant's product and checkout terms take precedence for a particular purchase.

Shipping and deductions

Original shipping charges, return shipping costs or reasonable deductions may apply where permitted by the relevant terms and law. If an item is faulty or incorrectly supplied, different remedies may apply.

Contact

For questions about a specific purchase, use the merchant or order contact shown at checkout. For general website questions, contact Wardrobe Alpha Edge at 3638 Westbourne Grove, London, UK, W2 5SH, GB, +1 2125559857.
